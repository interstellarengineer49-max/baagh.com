01 / OUR PHILOSOPHY

The most valuable thing you can make room for is life itself.

Baagh means garden. A place to put down roots, spend time together and reconnect with the things a busy week leaves behind.

A home is part of the story. So is the soil you tend, the food you learn to grow, the animals your children get to know and the simple pleasure of being outdoors. Baagh brings these possibilities together on land of your own.

04 / THE SETTING

Rooted in
the Deccan.

Set in Gajwel, Telangana, in the belt between Mallanna Sagar and Kondapochamma Sagar, Baagh belongs to the open landscape and changing seasons of the Deccan.

Gajwel connects to Hyderabad through the Rajiv Highway. Visit from your usual starting point, assess the approach and see how weekends, longer stays or everyday living could fit your routine.
